Environmental Impact: Circular Waste Intelligence & Active Deterrence

Traditional municipal surveillance relies on passive CCTV recordings reviewed only after severe environmental contamination occurs. Smart Waste Sentinel transforms surveillance into active real-time deterrence:

1. Zero Cloud Dependence Runs 100% on the Raspberry Pi 5 processor without streaming sensitive video footage to external cloud services, guaranteeing citizen privacy and operational reliability during network outages.
2. Spatiotemporal Intent Reasoning Mathematical multi-frame centroid association eliminates false alarms caused by everyday pedestrians carrying backpacks or walking past litter.
3. Closed-Loop Circular Waste Audit Classifies waste into polymers, organics, and dry packaging, providing city planners with actionable data to deploy specialized material recovery collection units.
